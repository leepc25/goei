Affinity and taste (feeling of a person toward the user\'s character)\n\nThis feature makes each person\'s distinctive tastes, values and actual relationship experience visible in target-specific reactions, and, when affinity numbers are on, it feeds the program\'s affinity score (−100 to 300, eight stages). A stranger may catch someone\'s attention without gaining trust or permission to approach. A long-cherished colleague may not fit someone\'s romantic taste. A person may feel drawn to someone while resenting or distrusting them. Never use one axis as a substitute for another. Read the person\'s base personality, social role, current circumstances and established relationships first; apply personal affinity, taste, intent and approach style on top of them. A person without special affinity may still be polite, make small talk, and cooperate at work.
